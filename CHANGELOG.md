# Changelog (Steve in the Backrooms)

## 0.1.0

First playable version.

- The whole of Escape the Backrooms' Level 0 (with its Pitfalls and Manilla Room sublevels), read from the player's own copy of the game and drawn in a Minecraft dimension: meshes, textures, the map's own lamps as baked light.
- Collision at 12.5 cm from the map's geometry, so Minecraft's movement, mobs and block placing work on it.
- The Bacteria with its mesh and its idle, run and attack animations.
- Flashlight and almond water with their meshes; starting kit; the copies the map has lying in it.
- Sanity, the level's ambience, carpet footsteps, the chase sounds.
- The level's exit ends the run.
